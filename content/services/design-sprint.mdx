# Design Sprint

A Design Sprint is one to four weeks of focused, senior design work scoped to your highest-priority design need. Brand identity refresh. UX audit of your product's core flow. A new visual system for a product you're redesigning. A suite of ad creatives for an upcoming campaign launch. The scope is set in kickoff — we don't start without clarity on what we're making and what it needs to accomplish.

Every Design Sprint is research-first. Before any visual work starts, we align on the user, the goal, and the constraint. Feedback loops happen every 2-3 days rather than at a formal mid-project review, which means small misalignments get caught before they become expensive direction changes.

Delivery is in Figma with handoff-ready specs — annotated components, export-ready assets, and a brief explaining every design decision for any future designer or developer working in the system. We don't drop a Figma file and disappear.

## What makes our approach different

We bring UX research methodology into design sprints that most agencies treat as purely visual projects. A brand identity refresh that doesn't account for how your current customers perceive the brand versus how prospects do is a visual exercise, not a strategy. A UX audit that doesn't include actual user behavior data is a set of opinions.

We've also built production design systems inside large product organizations, which means the visual system we deliver is structured to scale — component-based, documented, and buildable by a developer without a design translator on the call.
