# Google Ads Management

Google Search is still the highest-intent paid channel in existence. When someone types a specific query into Google, they're telling you exactly what they want. The problem is that most Google Ads accounts are structured to capture that intent inefficiently — broad match everywhere, keyword cannibalization, Performance Max campaigns absorbing budget without attribution clarity, and conversion tracking set up to fire on page views instead of actual revenue events.

Manu manages Google Search, Shopping, and Performance Max campaigns with a specific focus on conversion tracking integrity before anything else. If we can't verify that what fires in Google Ads actually represents a real conversion event tied to revenue, we fix that in week one. Running campaigns against inaccurate conversion data is the most common and most expensive mistake in Google Ads.

The engagement includes keyword strategy, ad copy and extension setup, weekly bid optimization, monthly reporting, and a strategy call. Three-month minimum. We build the campaign structure to be understandable — no black-box automation that you can't explain to your board.

## What makes our approach different

We bring the landing page into the conversation. Most Google Ads managers optimize the campaign and ignore what happens after the click. Manu works directly with Nishant on what the destination page needs to say and how it needs to perform for the ad spend to be worth anything. A 2-second LCP improvement on the landing page can do more for your cost-per-acquisition than two months of bid adjustments.

We also write reports that tell you the truth about what's working. If Search Impression Share is high and CPA is rising, we'll say so and tell you why.
