# AI Readiness Sprint

Everyone is being told to "add AI." Almost nobody is told where it actually pays off. The AI Readiness Sprint is one week of focused work to map exactly that for your business — which workflows are worth automating with an agent, which data is worth exposing through an MCP server, and which ideas are hype that will cost more than they return.

We start by auditing your tools, data, and the repetitive work your team does every week. Then we score each opportunity by effort and impact, recommend an architecture and model choice for the ones worth doing, and hand you a prioritized roadmap with rough costs. No 80-slide deck — a punch list you can act on.

## What makes our approach different

We build this stuff ourselves — custom MCP servers and production agents — so the roadmap is grounded in what actually ships, not in what sounds good in a strategy meeting. If something on your wishlist is a bad fit for an agent, we say so and tell you the cheaper way to solve it.

If you want, the first build on the roadmap can start immediately, with the sprint fee credited toward it.
