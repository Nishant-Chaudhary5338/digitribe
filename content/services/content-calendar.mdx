# Content Calendar + Social

Content that works is content that's aligned with what your paid acquisition is saying and what your ICP is searching for. Most brand content is neither. It's a mix of product announcements, industry news, and motivational quotes that nobody asked for, posted on an irregular schedule, and measured by vanity metrics that don't connect to pipeline or revenue.

We build a monthly content operation for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and email. The process starts with ICP messaging alignment — what problems are you solving, for whom, and what does that person need to believe before they buy? From that foundation, we build a monthly editorial calendar, copy briefs (you write with your voice, or we write for you), a distribution schedule, and a performance review at the end of each month.

Content is coordinated with paid. If Manu is running Meta campaigns that position you as the solution to a specific problem, the organic content reinforces that position. Everything tells the same story.

## What makes our approach different

We treat organic content as an owned media asset that compounds over time, not a social media maintenance task. That means we're building content around the search terms and conversation topics your ICP actually uses, so posts have a longer shelf life than 48 hours.

We also write performance reviews that measure what matters. Not impressions. Not follower growth. Inbound inquiries generated, qualified leads attributed to content-first touchpoints, and email list growth from content-driven CTAs.
