# Landing Page Sprint

A Landing Page Sprint is the fastest way to test a new offer, launch a campaign, or put a credible face on a product before you've built the full marketing site. Two weeks from kickoff to a live, Lighthouse-clean page — designed and built by the same people.

Most agencies separate design and development into sequential phases with handoff meetings in between. We don't. We design in Figma and review for build feasibility from day one. By the time the design is approved, 40% of the build is already scoped. That's how we hit two weeks without cutting corners.

The output is a single page — mobile-first, fast, and structured for conversion. Copy architecture is included in the brief. We set up basic analytics so you know who's landing and what they're doing. Two rounds of revision are built into the timeline. Post-launch, we're on call for two weeks to fix anything that surfaces in the wild.

## What makes our approach different

We treat landing pages as conversion experiments, not design deliverables. Before we open Figma, we ask: what action do you want the visitor to take, and what is the one thing that needs to be true for them to take it? Every section of the page is structured around that question. The hero, the social proof, the CTA placement — all of it answers back to the single conversion goal.

We also build pages that are genuinely fast. LCP under 2.5 seconds is a baseline. We've shipped pages with sub-1-second LCP on mobile. Page speed is not an afterthought — it's part of the brief.
