# Marketing Site

A marketing site is the most important asset most DTC and SaaS brands underinvest in. It's the one thing every prospect sees before they decide whether to trust you. A site that looks credible but loads slowly, or looks beautiful but buries the value proposition, is costing you conversions every day it's live.

We build 5-7 page marketing sites end-to-end: design in Figma, build in Next.js or Webflow (your choice), CMS integration if needed, analytics, sitemap, and OG images. The process runs 4-6 weeks depending on content readiness. We do the design before we touch code — no surprise pivots in week 5 because the layout doesn't work in the browser.

The scope includes: Home, About, Services or Products, Contact, and any service-specific landing pages agreed in the brief. We handle all responsive breakpoints, Core Web Vitals optimization, and basic SEO structure. Deployment is included. Thirty days of post-launch support is built into every project.

## What makes our approach different

Three things separate our marketing sites from the average agency output. First, we integrate design and development so you never wait three weeks for a "design handoff review." Second, every site we build is engineered for performance — not just designed to look good in a mockup. Third, we write the copy architecture before we design anything. The words determine the layout, not the other way around. Most agencies design first and ask the client to fill in the content later. We don't.
