# Automation Workflow

Most small teams are doing work every week that a well-designed automation could handle in 10 seconds. Weekly report compilation, lead qualification routing, client status email generation, social content repurposing, CRM data enrichment — this is work that costs you 3-8 hours a week per person and scales linearly with headcount.

We start with an automation audit: a structured review of your current processes to identify the highest-leverage workflows to automate first. Not everything is worth automating. Some things are too complex, too variable, or too important to delegate to a system. We're honest about that.

Once we've prioritized, we design and build the workflows — using Make (Integromat), n8n, Zapier, or custom API glue depending on what fits your stack. We integrate with your existing tools, document how everything works, and train your team to run and modify it themselves. We don't build automations that require us to be involved to maintain.

## What makes our approach different

Agencies that sell "AI automation" typically build one-size-fits-all workflows and call them custom. We start from your actual processes — we interview your team, look at your stack, and identify what's costing the most time. The output is specific to how you work, not a generic template with your logo on it.

We also care about the handoff. Every workflow we build comes with documentation, a simple test protocol, and a guide for your team to extend it. We've seen too many automations become black boxes that nobody touches because nobody knows how they work.
