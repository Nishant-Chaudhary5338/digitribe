# Shopify Build

Shopify stores that perform are not just configured — they're built. The difference between a store generating 3% conversion rate and one generating 1.2% often isn't the product. It's page speed, product detail clarity, cart UX, and checkout friction. We've seen brands cut bounce rate by 30% just by fixing LCP on the product detail page.

We handle custom theme development from scratch, performance-focused rebuilds of existing themes, and Shopify Plus migrations. The full SDLC: design in Figma, build in Liquid (or React via Hydrogen if you're going headless), app integrations, cart and checkout refinement, and speed optimization. LCP under 1.5 seconds is our target — not a stretch goal.

Our app integration experience covers the DTC stack: Klaviyo, Yotpo, Recharge, LoyaltyLion, Gorgias, and the standard Shopify Plus ecosystem. We know how to add these without destroying your theme's performance. We also know which apps to avoid when there are better native solutions.

## What makes our approach different

Most Shopify agencies specialize in theme customization, not engineering. We build Shopify stores with the same engineering discipline we apply to custom web apps. TypeScript where it's applicable, clean Liquid structure, accessibility baseline, and performance budgets enforced before launch. You get a codebase you can hand to a future developer without them spending two weeks reverse-engineering how it's structured.

We also bring a conversion-led design eye into every product build. Product detail pages, collection layouts, and cart UX are designed from conversion data, not from copying what every other DTC brand is doing.
