# Meta Ads Management

Meta Ads is the highest-leverage paid channel for most DTC brands and an increasingly important channel for SaaS brands targeting SMB buyers. It's also the easiest channel to waste budget on — creative fatigue, audience overlap, broad targeting that looks efficient in the dashboard but isn't driving real revenue.

Manu runs Meta campaigns at operator level. Campaign strategy, audience architecture, creative briefs, testing frameworks, weekly bid optimization, and monthly strategy calls. Three-month minimum engagement — not because we're locking you in, but because Meta campaigns take time to build proper learning history and we won't optimize against 10 days of data.

We handle creative strategy and briefs. If you have a creative team, we direct them. If you don't, we can connect you with trusted creative partners or help you produce simple but effective static and video assets. Attribution setup is included — if you're running Meta and don't have proper conversion tracking, you don't know your ROAS.

## What makes our approach different

Most paid social managers optimize for the metrics the platform shows them. Manu optimizes for the metrics your business cares about. That means connecting Meta Ads Manager data to actual revenue, not just attributed conversions, and having honest conversations when the attribution window is doing work that the revenue data isn't confirming.

He also operates with the teaching clarity he brings to his trainer role. You'll understand exactly what he's doing and why, so you're never dependent on us to interpret what the data means.
