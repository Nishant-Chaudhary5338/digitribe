# SEO Audit + Optimization

Most SEO audits generate 200-item reports that prioritize issues by severity rather than by business impact. You end up spending a week fixing meta descriptions and ignoring the fact that your three highest-traffic pages have 4-second LCP scores and aren't indexed in Google's rich results.

We audit differently. Three weeks, four layers: technical health (Core Web Vitals, crawl budget, indexation status, structured data), on-page signals (title tags, heading hierarchy, internal linking, canonical issues), content gaps (what you're not ranking for that your top three competitors are), and backlink profile (what's pointing at you and what's hurting you).

The deliverable is a prioritized punch list with effort/impact scoring — so you know what to fix this week, what to fix next quarter, and what to deprioritize entirely. We include a brief video walkthrough of every major finding. Implementation is an optional add-on: we can hand you the list, or we can fix it.

## What makes our approach different

We bring both engineering and marketing into the audit. Nishant reviews the technical layer — Core Web Vitals, crawl configuration, structured data markup — with the same rigor he brings to a performance build. Manu reviews the content and keyword layer against real paid search data, so you know which organic keywords are worth pursuing because there's already proven commercial intent behind them.

The audit is done by the people who will implement the fixes, which means the recommendations are scoped to what's actually achievable, not what's theoretically ideal.
