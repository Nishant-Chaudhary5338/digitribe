# Manu Siwach

Manu leads the Grow lane at Digitribe. He's a hands-on paid acquisition operator who has managed campaigns across Meta, Google, TikTok, and LinkedIn for brands at various stages of growth. He reads attribution reports for fun. He is not a media buyer who outsources optimization to platform algorithms and calls it strategy.

What makes him unusual is that he also trains people. He's an active trainer at one of India's leading digital marketing institutions, which means he spends a significant portion of his time breaking down exactly how campaigns work — bid strategies, audience architecture, creative iteration frameworks, attribution modeling — for people who need to understand it from first principles. That forces a level of clarity that most operators never develop.

He runs campaigns himself. Every client engagement gets his attention, not a junior's. He builds testing frameworks, writes creative briefs, sets up proper conversion tracking before a campaign goes live, and writes weekly performance reports that tell the truth about what's working and what isn't.

## What he believes about the work

Most paid acquisition is managed by people who learned what works on one account and apply it everywhere. The testing framework, the audience architecture, the bid strategy — it all looks the same across clients, regardless of what the data says. Manu builds the framework fresh for each brand based on what the attribution data actually shows.

He's also one of the rare paid media operators who understands the relationship between the ad and the landing page. He works directly with Nishant on what the page needs to say and how it needs to load for the ad spend to be worth anything.

## Philosophy

- Creative is the targeting. Audiences don't care about your demographics — they care about the hook.
- Proper conversion tracking isn't optional. Running campaigns without it is spending blindfolded.
- A weekly report that tells you everything is fine is usually telling you nothing useful.
