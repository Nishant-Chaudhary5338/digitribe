# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

## Who we are

Digitribe ("we", "our", "us") is a digital agency operating at **digitribe.world**. You can reach us at hello@digitribe.world.

## Data we collect

We collect the minimum data required to operate our service and respond to your inquiries.

- **Contact form submissions:** name, email address, company name, and message. Used only to respond to you.
- **Audit booking data:** name, email, and context provided via Cal.com at booking time.
- **Usage analytics:** page views, referrer, device type, and country — collected in aggregate, anonymously, via Plausible Analytics. No cookies. No personal identifiers.

## How we use your data

- To respond to inquiries and project briefs.
- To schedule and run audit sessions.
- To understand aggregate site usage patterns.
- We do not sell, share, or rent your personal data to any third party.
- We do not use your data to build advertising profiles or re-target you.

## Cookies

Our analytics tool (Plausible) is cookieless. We do not set tracking or advertising cookies. We store a cookie preference token in localStorage if you interact with our consent banner.

## Data retention

Contact form data is retained for up to 12 months and then deleted. Audit booking data is managed by Cal.com under their policy. Aggregate analytics data contains no personal identifiers.

## Your rights under GDPR

If you are in the EU or UK, you have the right to:

- **Access** a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your data.
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data.
- **Restriction** — limit how we process your data.
- **Portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Object** — object to processing for direct marketing purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@digitribe.world. We will respond within 30 days.
