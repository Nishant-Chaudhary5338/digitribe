# Full Stack

The Full Stack package is everything in Growth Engine, plus SEO and content operations, design support, quarterly strategy reviews, and a priority response SLA. It's built for brands that want to consolidate their digital partner stack to one team that owns the whole picture.

Most brands at the consolidation stage are managing three to five vendor relationships: a design agency, a development shop, a paid media buyer, a content person, and maybe an SEO consultant. Each vendor has partial context. Coordination between them costs time and creates gaps. The site doesn't reflect what the campaigns are saying. The content isn't aligned with the paid keywords. The design assets don't match what's live on the site.

Full Stack eliminates the coordination layer. Both founders are active on your account. Nishant handles build, design, AI, and technical SEO. Manu handles paid and content strategy. Monthly they review the full picture together and adjust. Quarterly they present a strategy review to your leadership team with what's working, what isn't, and what we're building next.

## What's included

Everything in Growth Engine, plus:

- SEO audit and ongoing on-page optimization
- Monthly content calendar (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, email)
- Design support: up to 8 hours of design work per month (ad creatives, landing page visual updates, email design)
- Quarterly strategy review with written report and leadership presentation
- Priority response SLA: responses within 4 business hours vs. standard 1 business day

## Who it's for

Brands that are currently managing multiple digital vendors and feel the coordination cost. Founders who want senior attention across all digital channels without hiring a full internal marketing team. Organizations moving through a growth phase where consistency across site, paid, organic, and design is critical to brand integrity.
