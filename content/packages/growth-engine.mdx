# Growth Engine

The Growth Engine is our most popular ongoing engagement. It combines Meta Ads management, Google Ads management, ongoing CRO work on the site, and monthly landing page A/B tests — all under one team, all coordinated to tell the same story.

This package exists because we kept watching DTC brands at the $5M-$20M ARR stage hit a paid acquisition ceiling. They'd maxed out easy wins on creative refresh and audience expansion. CPA was rising. ROAS was declining. The problem wasn't the campaigns — it was that the landing pages hadn't evolved alongside the creative, and the attribution wasn't sophisticated enough to tell them which channels were actually driving revenue.

The Growth Engine addresses the whole system. Manu runs the paid campaigns. Nishant handles ongoing site improvements and A/B tests to improve the post-click experience. Both of them work from the same data — so when a campaign angle is working, the landing page reflects it within days, not after a separate agency engagement.

## What's included

- Meta Ads management (strategy, creative briefs, weekly optimization)
- Google Ads management (search, shopping, PMax — with conversion tracking verification)
- Ongoing CRO work on key landing pages and conversion flows
- Monthly landing page A/B tests based on traffic volume
- Weekly performance reporting across both channels
- Monthly strategy call with Nishant and Manu together

## Who it's for

DTC brands doing $5M-$20M ARR who are running paid acquisition but aren't seeing the efficiency gains they expect at their spend level. SaaS brands with an established paid channel who want the landing page and campaign layers coordinated. Any brand that's currently split between a design/dev agency and a media buyer and experiencing the coordination cost of that.
