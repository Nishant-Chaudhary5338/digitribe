# Launch Sprint

The Launch Sprint is built for founders who need to go from no web presence (or a broken one) to a live, converting site with paid acquisition running — in 4-6 weeks. It combines our Marketing Site and Meta or Google Ads Setup into a coordinated launch under one team.

Most founders launching a new product or relaunching a brand make two mistakes. They hire a design/dev agency for the site and a separate media buyer for ads, and then nobody coordinates the two. The site says one thing and the ads say another. The landing pages aren't built around what the ads are promising. The conversion tracking isn't set up before the first dollar of ad spend goes out.

The Launch Sprint eliminates that coordination failure. Nishant designs and builds the site. Manu sets up the campaign architecture in parallel. By the time the site is live, the ad creative briefs are ready and conversion tracking is verified. Day one of launch is not the first day you're trying to figure out how this all fits together.

## What's included

- Full 5-7 page marketing site (design in Figma, build in Next.js or Webflow)
- Meta or Google Ads campaign setup and 30-day management post-launch
- Conversion tracking setup and verification before first campaign goes live
- Analytics, sitemap, and OG images
- 30 days post-launch site optimization
- All deliverables fully owned by you

## Who it's for

Founders launching a new brand or product who want site and acquisition coordinated from day one. DTC brands relaunching with a new positioning. SaaS founders preparing for a product launch who need a credibility-grade web presence and initial paid acquisition running before they announce.
